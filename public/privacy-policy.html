Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Gouseva Maatra Seva ("we", "us", "our") is a non-profit organization dedicated to cow welfare and protection. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our website (GousevaMaatraSeva.com) and our mobile app (together, the "Services").

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: your mobile number (used to sign in with a one-time password) and, if you provide them, your name and email address.
  • Donation information: donation amount, date, purpose, and order/transaction references. Payments are processed by Razorpay. We do not see or store your card, UPI, or bank details.
  • Contact information: details you submit through our contact form, such as your name, phone number, email, and message.
  • Technical information: basic data such as device type, browser, and a sign-in session token stored on your device so you stay logged in.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account and verify you by OTP.
  • To process donations, send receipts, and maintain donation records.
  • To respond to your enquiries and provide support.
  • To share updates about our work, where you have agreed to receive them.
  • To meet legal, accounting, and tax obligations, and to prevent fraud or misuse.

3. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with:

  • Payment processors (Razorpay) to complete your donation.
  • Service providers who help us run the Services (for example hosting and SMS/OTP delivery), under confidentiality obligations.
  • Authorities, where required by law.

4. Data retention

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above, including legal and accounting requirements (donation records may be retained for the period required by Indian law). You may request deletion as described below.

5. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures, including encrypted (HTTPS) connections, to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your rights and account deletion

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data and account by emailing us at the address below from your registered email or contact details. We will respond within a reasonable time. Records we are legally required to keep (such as donation receipts) may be retained.

7. Children's privacy

Our Services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Third-party links

Our Services may link to third-party sites (such as Google Maps or social media). We are not responsible for their privacy practices.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
